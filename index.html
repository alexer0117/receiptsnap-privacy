Privacy Policy for ReceiptSnap OCR

Last updated: November 2026

ReceiptSnap OCR ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use the ReceiptSnap OCR mobile application (the "App").

1. ZERO DATA COLLECTION & 100% ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

ReceiptSnap OCR is engineered from the ground up to be an offline-first, on-device utility:

  • Local OCR & Text Extraction: Receipt recognition, merchant parsing, and data extraction are performed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework. No receipt images, merchant details, or tax numbers are ever transmitted to any external servers or third-party AI APIs.
  • Local Database Storage: Your receipt records, scanned photos, expense categories, and exported financial reports are stored strictly in your device's local application sandbox (SQLite database). We do not operate remote servers to collect or store your personal financial records.

2. CAMERA & PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS

To provide receipt scanning and photo importing features, ReceiptSnap OCR requests access to your device's Camera and Photo Library:

  • The captured images and selected receipt photos are processed locally on your device only.
  • We do not access, analyze, or upload any other personal photos or data from your photo library.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & TRACKING

ReceiptSnap OCR does not integrate third-party advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or external behavioral analytics. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial information to any third party.

4. ICLOUD AUTO-BACKUP (OPTIONAL)

If you choose to enable the optional iCloud backup feature, your data is synced directly to your personal Apple iCloud container protected by Apple's encryption standards. We do not have access to your iCloud account or its data.
